# Desks

Instead of piling every window onto one screen, you get ten separate desks, each with a fixed number.
Put your writing on one and your browser on another.
Nothing on the desk you are not looking at can interrupt what is in front of you.

There is no setting up to do.
`Super+3` always goes to desk 3, whatever else is open. Desks are never renumbered, so you can learn where things live.

## Moving between them

| Keys | Goes to |
| --- | --- |
| `Super+1` to `Super+0` | that desk, by number |
| `Ctrl+Super+Left` and `Ctrl+Super+Right` | the desk to either side |
| `Super+PageUp` and `Super+PageDown` | the same, on a keyboard with those keys |

On a touchpad, four fingers left or right walks between desks.
Scrolling the wheel over the numbers at the top of the screen does the same.

The top strip shows the desks you are using, plus the one you are on, plus the next free desk (hover it and it says "New desk").
A desk with an app on it shows that app's icon; an empty one shows its number.
With work on desks 1, 2 and 5 you see three app icons and a 3 between them; click the 3, or press `Super+3`, to start a desk there.
Hold `Super` and every desk shows its number, so you can read off which key reaches it.
A desk joins the strip when something is on it or you go to it, and leaves again when it is empty and you are elsewhere.
Settings, Bar, "Show only the workspaces in use" turns this off and gives back the full row of ten.

## Sending a window somewhere else

`Super+Alt+1` to `Super+Alt+0` sends the window you are in to that desk and leaves you where you are.
`Super+Shift+PageUp` and `Super+Shift+PageDown` send it one desk over.

You can also throw it by hand.
Press `Super+Tab` for the overview, then drag any window onto any desk.

## Seeing all of it at once

`Super+Tab` shows your desks side by side, each one drawn the way you tiled it.
Only the desks in use appear, each under its own number, with a `+` for the next free one.
Four fingers up or down does the same.

Click a desk to go there, or click a window to go straight to it.
Drag a window onto another desk to move it there.
The arrow keys move between desks and `Enter` goes.
Start typing to find a window by its name, then `Enter` to jump to it.
`Escape` clears what you typed, and closes the overview when nothing is typed.
